About me

A web developer based in Malawi, building for small businesses and entrepreneurs across Africa and beyond.

I'm Francis Kelvin Mkanda, a web developer based in Malawi, working with small businesses and entrepreneurs across Africa and beyond. I build websites and web applications that do a real job for the people using them, not just look good in a screenshot.

Every project is built mobile-first, because that's how most customers will actually find you: on a phone, often on a slower connection. I develop with that reality in mind from the first line of code, not as an afterthought.

I also stay close to the work after launch. I test with real people and fix what breaks, because software only earns its value once people can actually use it.
